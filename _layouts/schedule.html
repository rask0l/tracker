Weekly rhythm // web exploitation // no deadline

The Study Loop

Two bugs a week. Learned deep, then written down from memory. It repeats for as long as it takes. Depth is the point, not speed.

  • Learn take it in
  • Consolidate prove it out
  • Catch-up flex day

// Every study day — same three moves

01 Warm-up · 10 min Anki — yesterday's payloads + syntax. The low-effort on-ramp. Starting is the only hard part.
02 Deep work · 25/5 Do-first: open the lab, attack, get stuck, then read to unblock. Ride hyperfocus when it shows.
03 Wind-down · 2 min Write tomorrow's exact first action. Leave Kali and Burp open for the morning.

01 — The week

01 First bug of the week
Mon
Learn · pt 1
~1–1.5h

Meet the new bug by breaking something — not by reading about it.

  • 10mAnki warm-up. Get in the chair.
  • 2×25mAttack the first labs of the module. Break it first, understand it second. [do-first]
  • 2mWind-down. Note where you stopped + the next section. Gym: Upper A if it's a lift day.
Tue
Learn · pt 2
~1–1.5h

Finish the bug. Push the labs where it actually gets hard.

  • 10mAnki warm-up.
  • 2–3×25mFinish the module's labs. The hard sections are where the learning is — don't skip them. [do-first]
  • 2mWind-down. Jot the 4–5 points you'd need to teach this bug tomorrow.
Wed
Consolidate
~1.5–2h

Turn what you saw into something you own — write it, then prove it.

  • 10mAnki warm-up.
  • 30–40mFeynman writeup — closed book first. Explain the bug like teaching a beginner: what it is, how to find it, how to exploit, how to fix. Then open the module to patch the gaps — every gap becomes a new Anki card. Keep it ugly and short, and publish it. [teach-it]
  • 45–60mThe box. Root a machine where this bug is the way in. Screenshot every step in report language. [apply]
  • 2mWind-down. Note what tripped you on the box. Gym rest or light.
02 Second bug of the week
Thu
Learn · pt 1
~1–1.5h

New bug, same move — attack first, read to unblock.

  • 10mAnki warm-up.
  • 2×25mAttack the first labs of the second module. [do-first]
  • 2mWind-down. Mark the stopping point. Gym: Upper B if it's a lift day.
Fri
Learn · pt 2
~1–1.5h

Finish the second bug's labs.

  • 10mAnki warm-up.
  • 2–3×25mFinish the module. Push the harder labs. [do-first]
  • 2mWind-down. Jot the teaching points for tomorrow.
Sat
Consolidate
~1.5–2h

Your biggest free block — write it up, then break a box with it.

  • 10mAnki warm-up.
  • 30–40mFeynman writeup — closed book first, then patch gaps from the module. Ugly, short, published. [teach-it]
  • 45–60mThe box. A machine where the second bug is the path in. Screenshot in report language. [apply]
  • —Body-double on a Discord call for the boring parts; go solo when you hit flow.
↺ Close the loop
Sun
Catch-up
~1–3h · flexes

The flex day. Repay the week's debt, bury what's shaky, reread your own words.

  • debtRepay debt. Short of your target hours on a weekday? Make them up now — capped at ~2h, and it resets tonight. Debt never rolls into next week. [cap it]
  • heavyHeavy Anki. A longer session than usual to bury anything that felt shaky this week.
  • reviewReread your week's writeups. Your own words back is the fastest, highest-retention review there is. [retrieval]
  • offCaught up, no debt? Light Anki, line up next week's two bugs, then the day is yours. Recovery is part of the plan, not a failure of it.

02 — Rules that keep it alive

  • 01Miss a day? Resume — don't double up. The streak breaking isn't the run breaking.
  • 02Protect the warm-up above all. Anki + one sprint still counts as a day.
  • 03Ride hyperfocus. The timer is for starting, not for interrupting flow.
  • 04Ugly and short wins. A rough writeup published beats a perfect one you never start.

Two bugs in. One loop closed. Run it again.
