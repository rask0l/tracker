    • Floor and ceiling. Six sprints is already a strong day. If you're empty after the gym, the 19:00 block is the part to cut, not the gym and not lunch.
    • The Anki trap. Eight weeks of backlog will show you a number in the hundreds, and that number alone can end the day before it starts. Set the limit to 20 first, do 15 minutes, close it. You are not clearing that backlog tomorrow.
    • Why it splits this way. New material goes on a fresh brain in the morning. After two hours of lifting you won't have the focus for new exploitation, but you will have enough for retrieval, and that lands inside the post-training window from rule 05.
